Under the hood

Paperwork in. Finished work out.

Your operations software and field scans already create PDFs. A document workflow picks them up and carries them the rest of the way, with a person checking anything it isn't sure about.

  1. 1CollectInvoices from your job software, receipts and inspection sheets from a desk scanner.
  2. 2Read and checkPull out the job number, customer, unit, and totals, then flag anything that doesn't match.
  3. 3File and syncRename and file every document, and push the record into QuickBooks Online.
  4. 4SendDraft the customer email with the invoice and backup attached, ready for your approval.

How it works

Built by someone who runs the back office.

Wraithlight Digital is run by an operator, not a sales team. The approach comes from doing the invoicing, books, and paperwork of a real small business week after week, and making that work take a fraction of the time.

Find the dark corners

A short walkthrough of your week to spot the tasks that are slow, repetitive, or always falling behind.

Build the light

A focused fix for the biggest time sink first, using tools you already pay for wherever possible.

Hand you the switch

Plain language instructions and a system you own. No lock in, no mystery boxes.

  • You own everything. Your accounts, your Microsoft tenant, your social profiles. We get access you can remove anytime.
  • You pay vendors directly. Software and scanners are bought in your name, with no markup from us.
  • Drafts before sends. New workflows prepare work for your approval until you trust them.
  • Your data stays yours. Business grade AI accounts that do not train on your information.

The goal is never "more AI." It is fewer late nights, fewer missed follow ups, and more time for the work only you can do.
